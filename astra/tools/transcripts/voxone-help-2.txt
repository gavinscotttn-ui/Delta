Keyboard Shortcuts for the Voxone app. Windows. If you press CTRL + F you focus on the search field (specifically used in the Contacts tab). While in a call, if you click on a colleague or use the arrow up or arrow down button to navigate between them. If you want to transfer a call to a certain colleague, you can use the following shortcuts: CTRL + A: Attended transfer to their landline number. CTRL + SHIFT + A: Attended transfer to their mobile number. CTRL + B: Blind transfer to their landline number. CTRL + SHIFT + B: Blind transfer to their mobile number. While on a call, you can also right-click on the contact to find the shortcuts. There is also a shortcut for answering and hanging up. This shortcut is CTRL + SHIFT + SPACE. Keep in mind that this shortcut needs to be activated to work. This is done via Options > Enable keyboard shortcuts. Mac.
